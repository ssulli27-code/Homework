Created Homework Question 
Lydia is planning a two-night trip to Boston and wants to figure out how much money she will need. She has already decided where she wants to stay, how she will get there, and one activity she wants to do. Create a Python program that stores the prices of her choices and determines the amount she will spend.  
Lydia is staying at the Downtown Hotel for two nights at $165 per night. Her train ticket costs $45. Her aquarium ticket costs $45. Massachusetts tax is 7%. Her dad gave her $25 for the trip to spend it on whatever she wants.  
Your program should include all the information listed. The summary should show her choices and all the costs that she has along with the final amount she needs to pay. 
 
I made this problem to test students’ ability to use the Python concepts we have learned in class to solve real world problems. Students will have to create constants and variables to store information about Lydia’s travel choices. They also need to use variables together to calculate the different variables. Finally, they will create a readable order summary using strings.  
The most challenging part will be to keep track of all the different variables and use the results of one calculation for the next one. Students will also need to make sure that numerical values are converted to strings in the final summary. They will also practice turning a word problem into Python code.
